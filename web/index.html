📐 Stance & Calibration Tips

  • Optimal Distance: Stand 1.5 - 2.5m (5 - 8 ft) from your webcam so your torso is framed. Watch the Distance OK beacon on the control dock.
  • Good Lighting: Ensure front lighting on your upper body. Avoid strong backlight behind you.
  • Standing Calibration: Press C while standing upright and centered to calibrate your baseline in 2 seconds.
