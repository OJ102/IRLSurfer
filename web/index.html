📏 Stance & Calibration Tips

  • Optimal Distance: Position yourself 1.5 - 2.5m (5 - 8 ft) from your webcam so your torso from head to hips is cleanly framed. Look at the Distance OK badge on the dock.
  • Lighting: Ensure front lighting illuminates your upper body. Avoid strong background sunlight or lamps directly behind you.
  • Baseline Calibration: Press C while standing centered and upright. Hold your neutral stance steady during the 2-second calibration sweep.
